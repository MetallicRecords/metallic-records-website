Legal Framework

Metallic Records · Effective/Updated: 3 September 2026

1. Purpose

This Legal Framework explains the relationship between Metallic Records' website policies, professional agreements, music rights, commercial transactions, and applicable South African law.

2. South African Legal Framework

Metallic Records operates subject to applicable law of the Republic of South Africa. Depending on the activity involved, relevant legislation may include the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008, Copyright Act 98 of 1978, Performers' Protection Act 11 of 1967, Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002, Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), and Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), together with applicable amendments, regulations, and other laws.

3. Consumer Transactions

The Consumer Protection Act establishes national norms and standards for consumer protection and addresses consumer information, unfair business practices, and consumer transactions and agreements. Metallic Records' commercial policies operate subject to mandatory consumer rights.

4. Music, Copyright and Performers

Music, sound recordings, compositions, artwork, performances, and related creative works may carry different rights. The Copyright Act regulates copyright in musical and other protected works, while the Performers' Protection Act addresses rights of performers. Specific ownership, licences, royalties, publishing, credits, and permissions must be determined from the applicable rights and written agreements.

5. Personal Information and Access to Information

POPIA provides requirements for lawful processing and protection of personal information. PAIA provides a framework for access to information, including records held by private bodies in circumstances provided by law. Metallic Records' Privacy Policy operates within these frameworks.

6. Electronic Transactions

Electronic transactions and communications may be subject to the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act and other applicable law. Website terms, electronic records, notices, payments, and online transactions must be interpreted consistently with mandatory legal requirements.

7. Metallic Records Agreements

Signed Metallic Records agreements may govern specific relationships with artists, producers, clients, licensors, or other professional parties. They may address recording, master ownership, royalties, publishing, production, beat licensing, deliverables, credits, confidentiality, exclusivity, termination, and other project-specific matters.

A private Metallic Records agreement or policy does not override a mandatory provision of South African law. Where an agreement conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the applicable law prevails to the extent of the conflict.

8. Relationship to Website Policies

The Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Refund Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy, and Terms & Conditions are separate policy documents. This Legal Framework provides the legal context for those documents; it is not a replacement for them.

9. Legal Review

This framework is a business-facing explanation of the legal structure and should not be treated as a substitute for advice from a qualified South African legal professional on a specific matter.
