Cookie Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, provide functionality and understand how visitors use a website.

2. How Metallic Records Uses Cookies

Metallic Records may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Help the website function correctly
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Improve the user experience
  • Support website security and technical functionality

3. Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for basic website functionality, security or technical operations.

Disabling certain essential cookies may affect the operation of some parts of the website.

4. Analytics Cookies

Where analytics services are enabled, cookies may help us understand how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics information may include pages visited, browser type, device information and general usage patterns.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services connected to the Metallic Records website may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

These services may include analytics providers, payment providers, embedded media platforms, social-media platforms and other external services.

Third-party cookies are controlled by the relevant provider and are subject to that provider's own policies.

6. Music and Embedded Content

Metallic Records may link to or embed content from music and streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music or other services.

These platforms may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

7. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control, block or delete cookies through browser settings.

You may choose to disable cookies, but doing so may affect certain website functionality.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

Metallic Records may update this Cookie Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact Metallic Records through the contact details provided on our website.
