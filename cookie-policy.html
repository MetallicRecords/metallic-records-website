Cookie Policy

Metallic Records · Effective/Updated: 3 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Metallic Records may use cookies and similar technologies on its website.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. They can help a website remember settings, operate essential functions, understand usage, or support embedded services.

2. Types We May Use

Essential cookies may be needed for website functions. Analytics cookies, where enabled, may help us understand general website usage. Third-party technologies may be used by embedded or external services such as music platforms.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Privacy

Cookie-related information is handled consistently with our Privacy Policy and applicable South African data-protection requirements.

5. Changes

Metallic Records may update this policy when website functionality, services, or legal requirements change.
