Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Digital Products

Metallic Records primarily provides digital music products, including MP3 files, WAV files, EPs and DJ Packs.

Because digital products may be delivered electronically, refunds may be limited once a digital product has been successfully delivered or accessed, subject to applicable South African consumer law.

2. Incorrect or Failed Delivery

If you experience a problem receiving a purchased digital product, please contact Metallic Records so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, provide the product again or resolve the issue.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us with your payment details so the transaction can be investigated.

4. Services

Refunds relating to recording, mixing, mastering, production, design or other services will depend on the work completed, the agreed terms and applicable South African law.

5. Contact

For refund enquiries, please contact Metallic Records through the official contact details provided on the website.

6. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or unlawfully restrict any consumer right that applies under South African law.
