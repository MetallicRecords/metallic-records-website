Refund Policy

Metallic Records · Effective/Updated: 3 September 2026

This Refund Policy applies to Metallic Records digital products and services, subject to applicable South African consumer law and the terms of any specific signed agreement.

1. Digital Music Products

Digital products may include MP3 or WAV releases, EPs, DJ Packs, beats, instrumentals, or other downloadable music products. Because digital content can be supplied immediately, refund eligibility may depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

2. Defective or Incomplete Files

If a purchased file is corrupted, materially defective, or incorrectly supplied, contact Metallic Records promptly with the order details. Where appropriate, we may correct the problem, replace the file, or provide another appropriate remedy.

3. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same digital product more than once, contact us with proof of the duplicate transaction. Any remedy will be assessed according to the circumstances and applicable law.

4. Services

Recording, production, mixing, mastering, distribution, promotion, and other services may have different cancellation, deposit, revision, or refund terms. Where a signed agreement exists, its applicable terms govern, subject to mandatory law.

5. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict a right that cannot lawfully be excluded under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 or other applicable South African law.

6. Payment Providers

Payment providers may have their own transaction procedures. This does not remove rights or remedies that apply between you and Metallic Records.

7. Requesting a Refund

Contact Metallic Records with your name, transaction details, product or service concerned, and the reason for the request.
