Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how Metallic Records handles refunds, cancellations and problems relating to products and services purchased through our website.

2. Digital Music Products

Metallic Records may sell digital music products including MP3 singles, WAV singles, EPs, WAV EPs, DJ Packs and other downloadable digital products.

Because digital products may be supplied electronically and can become accessible immediately after purchase, refunds for correctly delivered digital products may be limited once the digital product has been supplied or downloaded, subject to applicable South African consumer law.

3. Incorrect or Damaged Digital Files

If a purchased digital file is corrupted, incomplete, inaccessible or materially different from the product described, please contact Metallic Records as soon as possible.

Where appropriate, we may provide a replacement file or another reasonable remedy.

4. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same digital product more than once, contact us as soon as possible with the relevant transaction information.

Duplicate-payment requests will be reviewed individually and handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the transaction.

5. DJ Packs

DJ Packs are digital products. Once the pack has been successfully delivered or made available for download, refunds may be limited, subject to applicable law.

If the DJ Pack cannot be accessed or the delivered files are materially defective, contact us so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, provide a replacement or other suitable remedy.

6. Music and Production Services

Where Metallic Records provides recording, mixing, mastering, production, artist or other creative services, cancellation and refund arrangements may depend on the stage of the project and the specific agreement with the client.

Work already completed may not be refundable where applicable, subject to the client's rights under South African law.

7. Cancellations Before Service Begins

If a client requests cancellation before work has started, Metallic Records will review the request and determine whether a refund is applicable based on the agreed terms, payments already made and any costs already incurred.

8. Cancellations After Work Begins

If work has already started, any refund will take into consideration the work completed, resources used and costs reasonably incurred.

9. Payment Provider

Payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider. Where a refund is approved, the refund may be processed using the original payment method where reasonably possible.

Payment-provider processing times may vary and are outside the direct control of Metallic Records.

10. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include enough information for us to identify the transaction.

  • Customer name
  • Email address
  • Product or service purchased
  • Transaction or payment information
  • Reason for the refund request

11. Refund Review

Refund requests will be reviewed according to the nature of the purchase, the circumstances of the request, the applicable terms and applicable South African law.

12. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any consumer right or protection that cannot legally be excluded or limited under South African law.

13. Third-Party Products and Services

Where a purchase involves a third-party platform or service, additional refund rules may apply according to that provider's terms.

14. Changes to This Policy

Metallic Records may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website with an updated effective date.

15. Contact

For refund or cancellation enquiries, please contact Metallic Records through the contact details provided on our website.
