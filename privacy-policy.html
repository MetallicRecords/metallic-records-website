Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

1. Introduction

Metallic Records respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Metallic Records website, contact us, purchase products or services, or otherwise interact with Metallic Records.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect:

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Billing or transaction information
  • Information provided during enquiries
  • Information relating to music or production services
  • Technical information about website usage
  • Information voluntarily submitted through forms

3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected when you contact us, submit an enquiry, purchase a product, request a service, subscribe to communications, or interact with our website.

Certain technical information may also be collected automatically through cookies, analytics tools, server logs or similar technologies.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Process and manage orders
  • Deliver digital products
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide music and production services
  • Communicate about orders or projects
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud and protect website security
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Where a payment provider processes your transaction, your payment information is handled according to that provider's own privacy policy and security procedures.

Metallic Records does not require customers to send complete credit-card or debit-card details through email, social media or other unsecured communication channels.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for payment processing, music distribution, hosting, analytics, email, social media, streaming links and other website functions.

These third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Metallic Records may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website operate, remember preferences, understand website usage and improve performance.

For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

8. Marketing Communications

Where applicable, Metallic Records may send promotional or marketing communications where you have provided the required consent or where otherwise permitted by law.

You may request that we stop sending direct marketing communications where applicable.

9. Sharing Personal Information

Metallic Records does not sell personal information.

Personal information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to process payments, deliver services, operate the website, provide technical support, prevent fraud or comply with legal requirements.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information held by us.

You may also have rights regarding direct marketing and the processing of your personal information.

13. Protection of Personal Information

Metallic Records aims to process personal information in accordance with applicable South African privacy legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

14. External Websites

Our website may contain links to Spotify, Apple Music, social-media platforms, payment providers and other external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

15. Children's Information

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. Where applicable, information relating to minors should be provided by or with the involvement of a parent or lawful guardian.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Metallic Records may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website with an updated effective date.

17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please use the contact details provided on the Metallic Records website.
