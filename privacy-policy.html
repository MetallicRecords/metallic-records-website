Privacy Policy

Metallic Records · Effective/Updated: 3 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Metallic Records handles personal information when you use our website, contact us, purchase products, or request music and production services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with Metallic Records, we may receive your name, email address, telephone number, billing or transaction information, enquiries, service details, and technical information such as browser or device information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, process transactions, deliver products or services, administer artist and producer relationships, provide customer support, improve the website, maintain security, and communicate about services or releases where permitted by law.

3. Payments and Third Parties

Payment transactions may be processed by third-party payment providers. We do not needlessly collect or retain complete payment-card credentials where those details are handled by the payment provider.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We may use essential cookies and, where enabled, analytics or similar technologies to operate and improve the website. See the Cookie Policy for more information.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be disclosed to service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the business, process payments, deliver services, or comply with a legal obligation. Metallic Records does not sell personal information as a general business practice.

6. Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business requirements, or applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, and other lawful controls over your personal information. POPIA is the principal South African framework for protection of personal information, and the Information Regulator monitors and enforces POPIA and PAIA.

8. South African Law

This policy operates subject to applicable South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 and other applicable legislation.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries or requests, contact Metallic Records through the contact details published on the website.
