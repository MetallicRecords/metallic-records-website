Shipping Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

1. Digital Products

Metallic Records primarily provides digital music products and services, including MP3 files, WAV files, EPs and DJ Packs.

Digital products are not physically shipped. They are delivered electronically using the delivery method provided during or after the purchase process.

2. Digital Delivery

Customers are responsible for providing a correct email address or other information required for digital delivery.

If you have completed a purchase but cannot access your digital product, please contact Metallic Records so that we can investigate the issue and assist with delivery.

3. Physical Products

If Metallic Records offers physical products in the future, the applicable product page will provide the relevant shipping, delivery and collection information before purchase.

4. Delivery Delays

For digital products, delivery may occasionally be delayed because of technical problems, payment verification, email delivery issues or third-party service interruptions.

Where a delivery problem occurs, customers should contact us as soon as possible.

5. Incorrect Customer Information

Metallic Records is not responsible for delivery problems caused by incorrect or incomplete customer information supplied during the purchase process.

6. Third-Party Services

Some digital delivery or payment functions may rely on third-party platforms. Their availability and processing times may be outside Metallic Records' direct control.

7. Contact

If you experience a problem receiving a purchased digital product, please contact Metallic Records using the contact details provided on our website.
