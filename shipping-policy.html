Shipping Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Digital Products

Metallic Records currently provides digital music products, including MP3 files, WAV files, EPs and DJ Packs.

2. No Physical Shipping

Digital music products are delivered electronically. No physical shipping or delivery is required for these products.

3. Delivery

After a successful purchase, the applicable digital product will be made available through the delivery method provided by Metallic Records or its payment and digital-delivery systems.

4. Delivery Problems

If you have successfully paid but do not receive your digital product, please contact Metallic Records so that we can investigate and assist you.

5. Future Physical Products

If Metallic Records begins selling physical products in the future, separate shipping charges, delivery times and shipping terms will be provided before the purchase is completed.
