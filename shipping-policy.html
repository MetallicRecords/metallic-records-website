Shipping & Delivery Policy

Metallic Records · Effective/Updated: 3 September 2026

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how Metallic Records handles delivery of digital products and, where introduced, physical products.

1. Digital Delivery

Digital music, DJ Packs, beats, and other downloadable products are generally delivered electronically after successful payment through the method stated at checkout or supplied by Metallic Records.

2. Delivery Problems

If a download link does not work, a file is missing, or a product is materially defective, contact Metallic Records so that the issue can be investigated and corrected where appropriate.

3. Customer Information

You are responsible for providing accurate contact and delivery information where information is required to complete an order.

4. Physical Products

If Metallic Records introduces physical merchandise or other physical products, the applicable delivery method, charges, estimated timeframes, and any location restrictions will be stated for the relevant product or order.

5. Third-Party Services

Where a third-party platform is responsible for part of the delivery process, delays or technical issues may occur outside Metallic Records' direct control. We will take reasonable steps to assist.

6. Consumer Rights

This policy operates subject to applicable South African consumer-protection law and does not exclude mandatory rights.
